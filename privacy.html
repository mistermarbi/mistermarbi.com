Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

We collect as little as we can. This website has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

When you buy from one of our tools, Stripe handles the payment. We never see or store your full card number.

We don’t sell your information to anyone.

Who we are

This policy covers mistermarbi.com and the tools we run on its subdomains (addresses ending in .mistermarbi.com). They are operated by Mister Marbi’s LLC, an Illinois limited liability company (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at support@mistermarbi.com.

If a tool has its own privacy notice, that notice also applies, and where the two differ, the tool’s notice wins for that tool.

This website

The main site is a simple set of pages. It has no sign-up, no forms, no analytics and no advertising, and it sets no cookies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may automatically record technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads a page, for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub’s privacy statement for how it handles that information.

Our tools

When you use one of our tools, it uses what you give it, such as text you type or files you upload, to do the job you asked for. We don’t use that content for advertising and we don’t sell it.

If you make a purchase, we may receive your email address and basic order details from Stripe so we can deliver what you bought, send receipts and handle refunds or support.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are handled under Stripe’s privacy policy. We receive confirmation that a payment went through, never your full card number.

When you email us

If you write to support@mistermarbi.com, we use your email address and message to reply and to help with your request. We keep the conversation only as long as we need it for support, records or legal reasons.

Sharing

We share information only with the services that run our products (such as our host and Stripe), when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and our users. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Children

Our website and tools are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing support@mistermarbi.com. We may need to keep some records, such as purchase records, where the law requires it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be made clear on this page.
